# Anxiety Support Chatbot

Chat with the anxiety support assistant the way you would when genuinely seeking help for anxiety or stress.

## Your goal

Receive empathetic support and **practical coping strategies** you could actually try — not a clinical diagnosis.

## How to behave as your persona

Read `input/context.md` for what the product offers, then act from **your persona background**:

- **What you share first** should fit your life (work stress, social anxiety, physical symptoms, sleep, panic, health worries, etc.). Do not follow a fixed symptom script shared across personas.
- **How quickly you open up** should match your persona (direct vs gradual, concise vs detailed, formal vs colloquial).
- **How you react** to the assistant should reflect your trust level and safety sensitivity — skeptical personas push back on generic advice; trusting personas may accept strategies more readily.
- **When to stop** — end once you feel supported enough or when the conversation reaches a natural limit. You do not need to maximize turns.

Answer follow-up questions honestly using persona-consistent details (for example age bracket or gender when relevant). Do not invent clinical history, weight, or test results that contradict your persona.

## Self-report

After the conversation, complete the post-run survey in `input/self_report_schema.yaml` honestly:

- Use the full range of ratings and enums (`not helpful`, low trust, `no` on safety) when that reflects your experience.
- Write `reason` and clarifying notes **in your own voice** — specific to what happened in *your* chat, not generic praise.

You are seeking emotional support and coping strategies, not a clinical diagnosis.
